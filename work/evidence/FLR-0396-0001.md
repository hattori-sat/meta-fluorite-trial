# FLR-0396-0001 — Sequoia material-parity attempt and post-exit QMP evidence

## Verdict

Bounded negative/UNKNOWN experiment; the visual acceptance gate did **not**
pass. The patched Sequoia path reached its parameterized LIT READY marker,
24 binding records, and SUN setup, but the run faulted before a live QMP frame
was captured. The only full-frame QMP image is after Flutter had exited and is
uniformly black. Therefore whether colored Sequoia pixels appeared immediately
before the fault is **UNKNOWN**. This image is not proof that the live render
was black.

The separate HUD+Sequoia composition gate was not tested. FLR-0398 remains
gated on a valid colored-Sequoia result.

## Facts, inferences, hypotheses, and UNKNOWN

### Facts

- Patch 0334's parameterized material path and SUN marker were reached.
- Two present begins, one return, and a kernel Oops in `FEngine::loop` were
  observed during the same launch.
- The QMP still/video were captured after the app exited and are uniformly
  black.
- QEMU teardown passed.

### Inferences

- Material-interface parity alone has not established colored Sequoia output.
- The repeated present/Oops signature is consistent with FLR-0391, but this
  correlation does not prove the Oops caused the absent/unknown pixels.
- Native-only colored Sequoia and same-frame HUD+Sequoia composition remain
  independent gates. This run passes neither.

### Hypotheses

1. The Sequoia path may still fail before a visible frame despite material
   READY/binding.
2. A colored frame may exist before the recurring present/Oops fault; the
   missing live QMP capture prevents distinguishing this from (1).
3. The gate/log path mismatch independently caused the capture window to be
   missed.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether the Sequoia ROI contained any colored pixels while Flutter was live.
- Whether the expected native-above-parent visual-isolation state masked the
  HUD; there is no live frame for that check.
- The first-fault GDB stack/registers and the cause of the kernel Oops.
- Whether the present imbalance is causal or only correlated with the Oops.
