The host GNU binutils 2.38 printed repeated “Unrecognized DWARF form” warnings

The first source lookup used the wrong `ViewTarget` subdirectory; a bounded

output included `.pc` patch-history snapshots; follow-up searches excluded
`.pc` and `.git`. The Mini did not have `rg`; the manifest query was retried

## Judgment and verdict

- GPT-6.1 Sol reviewed the source/thread evidence read-only and concluded that
  the reachable unsynchronized `std::map` mutation is sufficient to justify a
  minimal fix. Recommended shape: one mutex for every map access; callback
  removes/copies the `shared_ptr<promise<void>>` under the mutex, then unlocks
  before fulfilling it; never hold the lock across Flutter messaging or a wait.
- The map race is a confirmed process defect. Its causal relation to this saved
  SIGSEGV is **UNKNOWN**; a fix and comparable runtime run must not be
  misreported as retrospective proof.
- FLR-0408 has no live QMP still/video. Sequoia and HUD pixels remain
  **UNKNOWN**, not black and not visible.
- Historical FLR-0277’s frame-event A/B did not establish causality and kept
  the map race open. The new evidence closes the reachable-schedule question,
  not the historical crash-causality question.
