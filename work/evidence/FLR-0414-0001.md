## Result

The ordinary app launch and live identity checks passed. Before any GDB process
was started, QMP showed the full 1280×800 frame with CPU/GPU/FPS HUD and Scenes
control in the upper region, while the 3D ROI `(x=0..1279, y=200..799)` was
uniform black: 768,000/768,000 pixels, luma 0. The pre-Oops still and all eight
pre-Oops frames had SHA-256
`d6293e5f4d1ed369e246fc836a4d917223627d6f42fe2b2abfcfa343656bc02f`.

At guest uptime 119.913246 the page fault began; the Oops was recorded at
119.915315. TID 697 (`FEngine::loop`) faulted at RIP and R10
`0x7fbf96e39541`, RSP `0x00007fbf38ad3750`, CR2 `0x0000000038ad3750`, with
EFLAGS `0x217`. The bytes at RIP begin `cf 83 ff 07`. The same-run executable
mapping was `7fbf96b54000-7fbf99d5f000 r-xp 00838000` for
`/usr/lib/libLLVM.so.18.1`; its Build-ID and the computed ELF VMA `0xb1d541`
match the earlier static candidate.

Present markers: begin `1`, return `0`, success `0`; shape-ready markers: `53`.
The captured post-Oops frame still shows the HUD and black 3D ROI, and matches
the pre-Oops frame byte-for-byte. All eight post-Oops frames also have the same
hash. Thus the capture is static across the sampled interval; it does not prove
an all-black full screen or a functioning 3D scene.

QEMU reported KVM disabled (`query-kvm: enabled=false, present=true`). TCG is
inferred from the QEMU 8.2.7 default in the absence of an explicit accelerator;
the active backend is not asserted as a directly measured fact.

## Debugger and cleanup boundary

- No guest GDB process was started and no hardware breakpoint was inserted.
- The post-Oops GDB preparation command only read the process mapping and
  computed the same runtime address. It ran too late to recover the entry
  transfer.
- Exact app stop and QMP quit passed; harness cleanup reported zero target and
  QMP processes. Independent postflight found no target owner, QMP socket, or
  occupied run ports. Candidate kernel/rootfs/qemuboot hashes were unchanged.
- This run did not prove the origin of control flow, an LLVM/QEMU root cause,
  or any product rendering success.
