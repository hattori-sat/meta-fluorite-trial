# FLR-0383 run 0001 — known material on production Sequoia

## Outcome

The blue UNLIT diagnostic material was constructed once and bound to 24
renderables across 2 selected Sequoia models (`READY=1`, `BUILD_FAILED=0`).
The liveness-bracketed QMP image shows the CPU/GPU HUD and Scenes control, but
the central 3D ROI is uniformly black. This proves material setup/binding, not
visible Sequoia geometry; the product visibility criterion failed.

## Failed attempt and fault classification

- The initial 45-second capture attempt is INVALID: the app had already exited
  with status 124; an empty PID made the original `ps` liveness check fail
  open. Its screenshot/video are not used to infer rendering. The defect and
  corrected fail-closed retry are recorded in the working log.
- Guest logs report two kernel Oops during the bounded run. `coredumpctl`
  recorded a 56.6 MB SIGSEGV core for PID 650 in the first attempt. Core
  attribution for the second, 120-second retry is UNKNOWN; do not infer that
  it caused the black ROI.
- Owned QMP quit was accepted; independent cleanup checks found no QEMU,
  runqemu, or flutter-auto process, no QMP socket, and no forwarded port
  listener. The Mini build filesystem retained 63 GiB free.
- Raw PPMs, guest logs, and QEMU artifacts remain at the Mini evidence role
  path `$BUILD_EVIDENCE/flr0383-0001/qemu/`. No QEMU image or raw PPM was
  copied to the Mac; PNG/MP4 were generated locally by streaming the QMP PPM
  bytes to the converter.

## Interpretation and next discriminator

Material creation and assignment are no longer the open question. Selected
renderables are bound, yet the central output is black. The current evidence
does not distinguish camera/visibility, draw output, present, or compositor
behavior, and does not justify blaming textures or lighting. Under FLR-0373,
compare Sequoia → a known control route (Planetarium) → Sequoia during one live
execution. Correlate each segment with scene-selection markers, live-bracketed
QMP captures, and the same HUD-excluded 3D ROI. Route activation alone is not
a visual success.
