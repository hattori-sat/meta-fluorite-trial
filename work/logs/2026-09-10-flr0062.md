## Entry 1 — static target/readback boundary audit (2026-09-10)

### Facts

- The registered Devtool source tree is Git-clean at the recipe's pinned
  Filament revision `2a86c0c60ecce9443fc34631570e924721b20b40`.
- `VulkanDriver::beginRenderPass` resolves the active
  `VulkanRenderTarget`, builds the render-pass and framebuffer keys, obtains
  the Vulkan framebuffer, begins the pass, and stores the target in
  `mCurrentRenderPass.renderTarget`.
- `VulkanDriver::draw2` emits `vkCmdDrawIndexed`, but its backend arguments do
  not carry an entity or render-target identifier; the active target is only
  available through `mCurrentRenderPass`.
- `VulkanDriver::readPixels` ends current command recording and passes the
  source target to `VulkanReadPixels::run`. The readback implementation copies
  `srcTarget->getColor0()` into a linear staging image, submits a separate
  queue operation, waits on its fence in `TaskHandler`, and invokes the client
  completion callback through the task's on-complete function.
- Existing layer diagnostics record readback entry, queue submit, fence wait,
  map, reshape, and task completion, but do not identify the source target's
  color image/view/format alongside the production draw boundary.

### Inferences

- H1 cannot be resolved by another global draw count: the backend has no
  entity identity at `draw2`, so the next useful correlation is the active
  target plus primitive handle at `draw`/`draw2`.
- H2 and H3 can be separated with an environment-gated trace at
  begin-render-pass, draw, driver readback entry, and Vulkan readback source
  attachment. This is smaller and more discriminating than changing render
  behavior or adding another offscreen target.

### Hypotheses

- H1: production draw commands use a different or unexpected target than the
  target passed to native readback. Prediction: target/image identifiers differ
  between the shaded draw pass and readback source.
- H2: the production target is valid but its color attachment is black or in an
  unexpected layout after draw. Prediction: matching target/image identifiers
  are present, but attachment metadata or a later target probe remains black.
- H3: the target contains pixels but the callback path loses the result.
  Prediction: readback source and fence/reshape complete, but the client result
  marker remains absent.
