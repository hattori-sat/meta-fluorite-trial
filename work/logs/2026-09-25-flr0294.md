### Inference

- The current diagnostic run reaches `FrameSkipper` and suppresses frame
  completion because the delayed fence is usually not linked to a submitted
  command fence. This explains the observed `beginFrame=false` flood and
  prevents treating the uniform QMP frame as a lighting verdict.
- The historical combined HUD/3D records remain valid counterevidence against
  the claim that Filament 3D or light rendering is generically unavailable.
  The current production Sequoia problem must be tested after the frame-loop
  boundary is separated.
