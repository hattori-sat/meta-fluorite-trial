## Plan

- Compare the production light path with the known-good self-made LIT fixture.
- Read only the relevant source/API call order and existing bounded markers.
- Do not patch until the first differing operation is identified.

## Initial facts

- FLR-0309 zero-intensity SUN reached queue/present and reproduced the same
  FEngine/libLLVM page fault as normal intensity.
- FLR-0308 no-light avoided the fault but stalled before present.
- FLR-0286 self-made LIT fixture produced colored native 3D plus HUD in QMP.

## Current state

The source/API comparison found the first controlled order difference:

- production `DrawFrame()` calls `ecs->update()` and then
  `setupProductionProbeLight()`;
- the known-good fixture creates its SUN while constructing the direct
  diagnostic scene, before its render loop;
- production model/material work is driven through the ECS update path;
- the fixture creates its own geometry, material instance, and SUN in one
  direct scene setup path.

The smallest next discriminator is therefore to move only the opt-in
production light setup before `ecs->update()` while keeping the default order
unchanged.
