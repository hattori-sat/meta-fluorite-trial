# FLR-0028 working log — production shape+light co-enabled boundary

## Plan

- Reuse the FLR-0027 rootfs/profile, fixed Mini PC receiver/build/TMPDIR, and single QEMU evidence root.
- Run one co-enabled production condition with only model and environment skipped.
- Capture QMP-only early/late photographs and stop QEMU through QMP.

## Facts

- No QEMU, Flutter, Weston, or socat process was present before startup.
- QEMU used qemux86-64, TCG multi-thread, 2048 MiB, 4 vCPU, virtio-vga 720x400, rootfs snapshot, and the FLR-0027 rootfs.
- Launch identity was `Application Id: fluorite`; the installed Example Demo bundle was explicitly launched as `agl-driver`.
- Runtime flags were `FLR0026_NATIVE_SKIP_MODEL_LOAD=1`, `FLR0026_NATIVE_SKIP_ENVIRONMENT=1`, `FLR0026_SYNC_TRACE=1`, and `FLR0026_FORCE_RENDER_ON_SKIPPED_FRAME=1`. Shape/light skip flags were not set.
- The log reported 37 shapes with `renderable=true`. Vulkan surface creation, swapchain creation, acquire, queue submit, and `FLR0026_VK_QUEUE_PRESENT_BEGIN` were observed.
- Immediately after `FLR0026_VK_QUEUE_PRESENT_BEGIN`, the serial reported a page fault while `Comm: FEngine::loop` was active. The QMP frame remained HUD-only with no native geometry.
- Early and late QMP PPMs were identical: 720x400, SHA-256 `32caad392fafe6cc72198839fb177621f5667e94d3614c62d58499e9de8c676d`; candidate region `[200,100,400,250]` changed `1268/100000`, bbox `[200,113,29,86]`.
- QMP `qmp_capabilities` followed by `quit` returned `reason=host-qmp-quit`; the socket and associated processes were absent afterward.

## Inferences

- Shape setup and the common Vulkan path are not the first failure in the co-enabled condition.
- The first observed divergence is after present begins, before a stable native 3D buffer reaches the QMP framebuffer.
- The 2D route remains healthy enough to show the HUD, so the result is not a general Flutter/QMP display failure.

## Hypotheses

1. A userspace FEngine/Filament or Vulkan WSI fault occurs at or immediately after `vkQueuePresentKHR`.
2. The serial page-fault report needs userspace symbol/core correlation before ownership can be assigned.
3. The shape/light command interaction is the trigger, while each isolated FLR-0027 control remains valid.
