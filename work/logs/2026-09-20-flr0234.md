- QMP metrics: initial HUD `2801`, native `24178`; post-hover HUD `0`, native

- Runtime selected log: Vulkan queue present `result=0`, parent and native
  surface commits continue, and app/QMP teardown passed with zero residual

## Inferences

- The 3D producer and native child composition are not the immediate fault in
  this unit. The unchanged native ROI and motion-skip A/B support that split.
- The observed white pixels are limited to the measured top-right HUD ROI,
  which is the `Scenes` control rather than the full 2D/3D framebuffer.
- The default Material hover overlay is a lower-risk correction than changing
  embedder event phases or suppressing motion globally.

## Hypotheses

1. The default `FilledButton` hover overlay paints the uniform white ROI.
2. Flutter parent repaint remains opaque even when the button overlay is
   transparent.
3. The Wayland parent import path independently turns the button ROI white.
