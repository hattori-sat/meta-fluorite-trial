## Do / Check

### Inference

The first proven divergence is after native-surface attachment and before a
stable Filament frame can be started. The current frame is therefore not a
valid light/material verdict, and it is not evidence that the vehicle is
black. The identical FLR-0289/0291 frame hash shows that the translucent
diagnostic control is reproducing the same lifecycle failure rather than
providing a new visual result.

### UNKNOWN

- The exact Vulkan acquire/swapchain status returned by the underlying
  `beginFrame` rejection is not yet exposed by the current trace markers.
- The historical FLR-0066/0070 combined frames used different controlled
  runtime profiles; their later non-reproduction means they are historical
  discriminators, not a stable current production baseline.

## Act

Do not create a new surface or lighting patch. The next discriminator must
expose the underlying swapchain/acquire rejection or compare the exact
FLR-0070 one-light profile against the current beginFrame lifecycle, one
variable at a time.
