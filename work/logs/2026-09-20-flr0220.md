## Inferences

- RGB-positive bytes alone are insufficient evidence of 3D geometry.
- Clear/placeholder output is falsified for the minimal fixture.
- Readback conversion producing uniform white is falsified for the minimal
  fixture because the target contains 111,758 chromatic pixels.
- The first unresolved 3D boundary is final Wayland/native-surface composition
  after a real geometry-containing target.

## Hypotheses

1. The compositor imports a different, empty, or rejected buffer even though
   the native target contains geometry.
2. The native surface is committed but hidden by parent/child stacking, alpha,
   position, or output selection.
3. QMP/scanout captures a different final surface than the compositor path.
