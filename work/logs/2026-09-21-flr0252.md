does not prove production 3D. The production central ROI remains effectively
black and Scenes does not open. The next loop must classify the repeated Dart
call timeout before another source patch.

## Inferences

- The QMP coordinate is not the first failure: the pointer reached the Flutter

  Sequoia pixels in this current image/profile.

1. A readiness MethodChannel call is timing out and preventing route handling.
2. The production asset stage is stalled before visible resource/render work.
3. The native surface presents a transparent/black frame after creation even

## Iteration 2 — GDB/source correlation

### Facts

- A single production QEMU was launched with the fixed rootfs/kernel/profile,
  inspected with guest GDB, and then terminated through the fixed QMP socket.
- GDB attached successfully to `flutter-auto` and recorded 38 threads.
- One `flutter-auto` thread was blocked in libc++
  `std::__1::__assoc_sub_state::wait` (`future.cpp`); the application frames
  were unresolved. Vulkan queue, Filament engine, and llvmpipe worker threads
  were present and waiting normally.
- The application executable was `/usr/bin/flutter-auto`, Build ID
  `ee79c025e56898da98c1ad4f7cdea21abc568e8b` as reported by the guest probe;
  no matching `/usr/lib/debug` file was found. System, Mesa, and LLVM symbols
  were available.
- QMP-only capture `gdb-settled.ppm` reproduced the prior frame hash
  `98fefc82310d2c9ab2ae8decfb55a19bcaca5d4d17d506899cc37172c4bfa09e`.
  The HUD ROI had `2845` chromatic pixels; the production ROI had `0`
  chromatic pixels and only `116` changed grayscale pixels.
- QMP teardown reported `qmp=PASS capabilities=negotiated quit=accepted` and
  `cleanup=PASS residual_targets=0 residual_qmp=0`.

### Static source evidence

- The persistent Mac Devtool source has a blocking `scriptFuture.wait()` at
  the end of `ViewTarget::DrawFrame`.
- The Dart `FrameEventChannel` awaits `filamentViewApi.drainFrameTasks()` in
  `_onPreRenderFrame` before the method-channel reply is returned.
- The current source also contains the earlier software-frame scheduling fix;
  therefore this observation does not justify reapplying the old frame-promise
  patch.

### Inference

The strongest current lead is the native↔Dart frame-event completion boundary.
This is a correlation between a stripped application stack and the source,
not a proven exact frame. Asset loading and Wayland composition remain
separate hypotheses.
