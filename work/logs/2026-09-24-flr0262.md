## Inference

The first observed loss is after native swapchain readback and before the
QMP-visible candidate. This supports a Wayland child-surface or composition
boundary problem, but whole-image readback statistics do not yet prove that
the intended production car geometry is present in the native buffer.

## Hypotheses

1. Native readback contains the production scene, but the surface presented to
   Wayland/QEMU is stale or black.
2. Native readback contains only nonzero 2D/background content, while the
   production 3D region is also black.
3. The readback coordinate/content contract differs from the visible surface.
