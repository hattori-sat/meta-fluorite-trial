### Inferences

- The existing Sequoia code does not yet use the current-image LIT constant
  positive, despite sharing LIT shading and nominal blue RGB with a different
  historical parameterized fixture.
- Swapping only the dynamic shader color input for the proven literal blue is
  a narrower test than changing original GLB textures, camera, or composition.
- The source and runtime binding gates prove the diagnostic constant material
  reached all 24 selected renderables. They do not prove Filament drew or
  presented those primitives.
- The post-READY live QMP frame proves that the HUD and Scenes control were
  visible while the Sequoia region remained black. Because the present loop
  had an unmatched second call and a repeated `FEngine::loop` Oops, this is a
  snapshot of a faulted live run, not a healthy-render material verdict.

### Hypotheses

1. The constant-source LIT material can produce Sequoia pixels once the
   renderer reaches a healthy repeated-present state. Current run did not
   establish this because only one present returned and the FEngine loop
   Oopsed.
2. A shared renderer/present fault prevents visible native pixels despite the
   material and SUN setup. The recurring unmatched present/Oops supports this
   as the leading boundary, but causality is UNKNOWN.
3. Sequoia-specific asset/renderable state still fails to produce pixels even
   with the constant material. This remains possible and requires a same-image
   known-positive fixture control.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether the exact current candidate renders Sequoia with the constant LIT
  material during a healthy present loop.
- Whether the Oops causes the missing native pixels or is only correlated
  with them.
- Whether a generic known-positive LIT/SUN fixture still renders with HUD on
  the exact FLR-0391 image.
- The cause of the recurring FEngine/libLLVM page fault. Prior FLR-0366 GDB
  observed no signal and could not identify the invalid access; FLR-0339's
