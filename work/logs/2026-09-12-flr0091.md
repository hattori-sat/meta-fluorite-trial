## Facts

- llvmpipe workers wait inside `libvulkan_lvp.so` before fence signaling.
- The native QMP region is zero while the 2D HUD is visible.
- Production command trace: 1,415 begin / 1,414 done markers; the sole
  unmatched operation is `bindPipeline` after completed render-pass setup,
  scissor, and descriptor-set binds.
- Production pipeline trace: the final `GRAPHICS_PIPELINE_CREATE_BEGIN` has no
  create-done, cache-entry, or command-bind completion marker. The source maps
  this to `VulkanPipelineCache::createPipeline` around
  `vkCreateGraphicsPipelines`.
- Self-made fixture trace: 2,770 begin / 2,769 done markers; the unmatched
  final `commit` occurs at capture shutdown after 41,750 native-region pixels,
  6,246 HUD-region pixels, repeated submit/present markers, and 20 QMP frames
  were already captured.
- Production QMP: native `0/223200`, HUD `116/100000`.
- Production and fixture runs completed QMP quit and residual cleanup with
  zero remaining QEMU/QMP targets.

## Inferences

- Command/resource execution is the next boundary to isolate.
- The production-only divergence is inside graphics-pipeline creation, before
  draw/submit/present completion. This explains the production black native
  region more directly than alpha or surface placement.

## Hypotheses

1. A production shader/resource/layout/render-pass combination blocks llvmpipe
   during `vkCreateGraphicsPipelines`.
2. A production resource lifetime or pipeline-cache key is invalid.
3. The observed boundary is an instrumentation/timeout artifact; this remains
   possible until a runtime backtrace or a controlled pipeline-input cut is
   collected.

## UNKNOWN

  or accessing an invalid resource.
- Whether the guest journal/syscall path records a fault.
