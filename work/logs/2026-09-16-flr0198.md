## Facts, inferences, hypotheses, and UNKNOWN

### Facts

- The Devtool source, official patch, bundle, Mini recipe/build/image gates,
  and QEMU lifecycle are all reproducible and passed for this ticket.
- The driver-completion readback buffer has non-zero bytes.
- The QMP-visible HUD is present while the fixed 3D candidate is black.

### Inference

The first missing visible-pixel boundary is downstream of the Vulkan driver
completion statistic, or the non-zero bytes are outside the 3D candidate. The
result is not sufficient to claim that 3D geometry reached the visible ROI.

### Hypotheses

1. The native child surface has non-zero content but is unmapped, misplaced,
   scaled, alpha-hidden, occluded, or otherwise not included in the QMP-visible
   composition.
2. The driver buffer's non-zero bytes belong to another region or clear/HUD
   content; the 3D candidate itself is still empty.
3. QMP captures the Flutter parent surface but not the native child surface's
   compositor path.

- Native child-surface geometry, buffer scale/viewport, opaque/input region,
  and compositor stacking at this exact image are not yet correlated with the
  QMP frame.
