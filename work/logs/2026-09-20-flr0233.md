## Static Analysis

`Display::pointer_handle_motion` updates surface coordinates, chooses
`kHover`/`kMove`, and calls `Engine::CoalesceMouseEvent`. The gate was placed
only around that delivery block. The A/B result confirms that this event
consumer boundary is causally upstream of the white parent transition.
