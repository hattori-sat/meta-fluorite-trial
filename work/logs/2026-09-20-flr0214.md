## Inferences

- The producer generated RGB and the compositor can read the same RGB-positive
  shared buffer. The “RGB disappears before `wl_surface.commit`” hypothesis is
  falsified for this run.
- The QMP black result is now evidence of a missing final composition step,
  not evidence that the 3D renderer generated a black image.
- Prior alpha, flush, stacking-direction, clipping, and layout A/Bs do not
  identify the owner. The next evidence must expose compositor surface role,
  parent/child relation, geometry, and selection state.

## Hypotheses

1. The native surface is imported but not selected because its parent/child role
   or stacking relation differs from the known-good SHM surface.
2. A surface visibility/geometry/role property suppresses the native surface
   after import.
3. A compositor-side format/metadata rule suppresses the surface even though
   mapped RGB is present; lower probability, still UNKNOWN.
