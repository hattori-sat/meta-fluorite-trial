### Inferences

- The post-checkout local.conf grep can reject after the receiver has already
  moved; it is not a safe preflight and is not authoritative for effective
  BitBake values.
- Target-less `bitbake -e -T 5` is the best-supported deterministic source for the
  effective `TMPDIR`. The helper must compare it exactly with the fixed
  `BUILD_TMPDIR` role argument and confirm `TOPDIR` is the selected build.
- The location of copied/staged QEMU inputs is not a valid proxy for BitBake's
  `TMPDIR`.
- A missing local process after a helper invocation is only a liveness fact;
  it is not evidence that the receiver reached the requested revision.
- The remote-MCP role config cannot safely substitute for the Mini build-role
  configuration, even though both describe SSH-based remote systems.
