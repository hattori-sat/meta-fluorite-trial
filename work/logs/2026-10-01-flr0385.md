## Facts, inference, hypotheses, UNKNOWN

### Facts

- Source patch 0332 already assigns the proven FLR-0371 parameterized LIT/RGB
  material to all selected Sequoia primitive slots.
- A4 proves runtime material construction/binding and a live HUD screenshot;
  it does not prove a draw from the bound material reached visible pixels.
- The Oops precedes the LIT material READY marker. A4 therefore is not a
  healthy material-only negative. The eight frames are identical.
- Profile B was skipped because the required healthy-A precondition was false.

### Inferences

- LIT binding is no longer the earliest observed failure point. The missing
  present return/Oops occurs earlier, so changing only SUN or material would
  not be an evidence-based next step.
- This A4 screen does not establish a texture, camera, light, or compositor
  root cause. The fault and absent pixels are correlated, not causal proof.

### Hypotheses

1. A shared current-image renderer/present fault also breaks the known fixture;
   predicted result: the exact-image fixture control faults before repeated
   successful presents and its native ROI remains black.
2. The production Sequoia asset/load/draw path triggers the fault;
   predicted result: the exact-image fixture control renders with repeated
   successful presents while Sequoia A4 does not.
3. Draw output can complete while a later native surface/present/composition
   boundary loses it; predicted result: a healthy fixture/scene shows positive
   draw records but still has no native QMP pixels.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether a draw using the Sequoia LIT material completed before the renderer
  thread faulted; the ephemeral full app log was not preserved.
- Whether the Oops caused the missing Sequoia pixels, is a production-path
  trigger, or is concurrent; exact faulting access and root cause remain
  unknown.
