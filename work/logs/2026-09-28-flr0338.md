- The 0338 success measure allowed either an exact breakpoint hit or a
  bounded no-hit result. The breakpoint was confirmed armed and observed for
  the full bound, so this experiment is complete; whether the runtime ever
  executes the target remains UNKNOWN.

  retry: the Present-enter marker is reached, its return/done markers are
  absent, and the visible 3D ROI stays black. This does not establish whether
  the Present call blocks, an FEngine worker faults, or app-side synchronization
  is involved.

### Hypotheses

1. **Vulkan WSI/driver Present wait:** a bounded stack at the stalled window
   should place an FEngine worker inside the Vulkan Present/driver path.
2. **LLVM/FEngine worker fault or exit:** bounded kernel/app evidence should
   show an Oops or the issuing worker absent while the parent survives.
3. **Filament/app synchronization:** the relevant thread should instead be
   waiting on an app-side mutex/futex/semaphore or queue handoff before/around
   the Vulkan dispatch.

  a different worker fault/lock causes the missing return.
- Whether the ready/applied emissive binding is sampled by the runtime shader.
- Whether the anomalous HUD timing metrics reflect real frame delay or bad
