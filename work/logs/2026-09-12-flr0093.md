  `GRAPHICS_PIPELINE_CREATE_BEGIN`.

  passing.

  GDB once to the single QEMU process. It saw 39 threads; llvmpipe and

  captured.

  `0/223200`, HUD `1284/100000`; teardown passed.

  `vkCreateGraphicsPipelines`. The effective inputs include shader modules,

- The authoritative Mini `filament-vk:do_patch` gate passed with 104 tasks,

  `[200,113,29,66]`. QMP quit and residual cleanup passed.
- The runtime emitted six `FLUORITE_VK_PIPELINE_INPUT` records and six
  `FLUORITE_VK_PIPELINE_CREATE_RESULT result=0` records. The slowest create

- Evidence manifest: `work/evidence/FLR-0093-pipeline-input-qemu-2026-09-12.md`.
- The first post-change `make verify` found only a stale baseline lock:

## Inferences

- The next cut must stay below Filament's pipeline-cache call and above the
  guest driver/runtime state.
- The first missing create-done marker was not a proven permanent deadlock:
  the same class of calls completed in the longer observation. The current
  persistent boundary is fence completion/present return.

## Hypotheses

1. Production shader/resource work is expensive enough to create llvmpipe
   timing or resource pressure.
2. Production pipeline/resource inputs leave queue work pending and prevent
   fence completion.
3. The apparent create boundary is a capture-timing artifact and the real
   defect is the later synchronization path.
