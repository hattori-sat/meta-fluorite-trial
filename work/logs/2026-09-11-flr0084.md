## Plan

Refresh the persistent Devtool source, add only opt-in lifecycle markers around
the driver thread and queue wait, generate the official layer patch, and prove
the marker boundary on the Mini image before considering a product change.

## Facts

- The missing boundary is before Vulkan present and before current-image QMP
  pixels.

## Inferences

  production-light or compositor A/B.

## Hypotheses

- Driver thread never reaches loop.
- Driver thread blocks at queue wait.
- The running binary is not the expected patched instance.

  and was later reused by diagnostic flags and evidence paths. It is now
  legacy-only: no new directory, flag, marker, or log will use it.
