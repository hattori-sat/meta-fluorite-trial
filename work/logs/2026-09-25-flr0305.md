and attaches a light to the FilamentSystem default Scene, while
`addLoadedLightsToScene()` is explicitly diagnostic-Scene-only. The existing

`(0.7,-1,-0.8)`.

to the default Scene from `ViewTarget`, stores its entity for destruction, and
does not change model, camera, culling, material, or surface behavior.
