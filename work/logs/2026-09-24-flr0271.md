## Check

### Facts

- QMP can capture the direct native fixture and the 2D HUD simultaneously on
  the same image and profile.
- Therefore the QMP framebuffer omission hypothesis is falsified for this
  profile.
- The readback path still has a missing boundary between accepted readback
  content and composed QMP pixels.
- One fixture run crashed in llvmpipe while another retained a visible frame;
  fixture runtime stability is nondeterministic and remains UNKNOWN.

### Inferences

- The direct SHM/native fixture path is capable of producing actual screen
  pixels; a source-side vertex/material/camera absence is not the explanation
  for the fixture black window.
- The readback failure remains narrowed to compositor surface-tree/SHM import
  behavior, not QMP capture.

### Hypotheses remaining

1. The readback child is accepted by the client protocol but is absent from
   the compositor's effective visible tree or stacking order.
2. The compositor imports the child surface but rejects or mishandles the
   SHM buffer contents without a protocol error.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether the production Planetarium/Sequoia route produces pixels on the
  current image.
- Whether a route transition reaches the intended scene callback and camera/
  light setup on the current image.
- Whether the llvmpipe crash is deterministic or specific to the 0292 timing.
