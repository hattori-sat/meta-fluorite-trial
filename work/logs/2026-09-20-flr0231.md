# FLR-0231 working log — isolate button-release repaint

Date: 2026-09-20
Ticket: FLR-0231
Status: In Progress

## Outcome

Opened after FLR-0229 partially confirmed the output-enter metrics hypothesis.
The remaining white transition is now isolated to a later button-release path
when the output-enter metrics resend is disabled.

## Facts

- Metrics-skip initial frame: native 3D ROI `chromatic_pixels=24178`; HUD ROI
  `chromatic_pixels=2801`.
- Metrics-skip `move-y`: native ROI remains `24178`; HUD remains chromatic
  (`chromatic_pixels=2799`).
- Metrics-skip `up`: HUD becomes uniform white; native ROI remains unchanged.
- The explicit output-enter skip marker was emitted and QMP teardown passed.

## Hypotheses

1. Button-up delivery triggers the remaining full parent repaint.
2. Wayland damage/import is independent of button-up delivery.
3. Native child ordering changes on release; current evidence makes this low
   probability.

## Static analysis

- The effective Mini source maps `wl_pointer.button` to
  `Display::pointer_handle_button` in `shell/wayland/display.cc`.
- The handler records `button`, `state`, and `serial`, updates the Flutter
  button mask, maps `WL_POINTER_BUTTON_STATE_RELEASED` to
  `FlutterPointerPhase::kUp`, and calls
  `Engine::CoalesceMouseEvent(kUp, ...)`.
- `Engine::CoalesceMouseEvent` only constructs a `FlutterPointerEvent` and
  appends it to `m_pointer_events`; this path does not directly call
  `WaylandWindow::handle_base_surface_enter`, native scene setup, or a Vulkan
  present operation.
- Therefore the current discriminator boundary is the delivery of the
  button-up event versus an independent parent-surface damage/import path.
- The source baseline used for this inspection is the Mini build's effective
  post-`do_patch` Flutter source; the Mac Devtool baseline is the matching
  self-contained source commit documented by FLR-0229.
