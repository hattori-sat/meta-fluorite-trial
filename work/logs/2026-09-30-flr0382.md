### Inferences

- The stage-trace toggle is the smallest documented difference that directly
  tests the silhouette-versus-gray observation without reopening texture,
  camera, or lighting hypotheses.
- A silhouette recurrence would identify an association only. The trace emits
  high-volume records and may affect timing, so raw log volume and liveness
  must be preserved as separate evidence.
- The `SCENE_ADD_DONE` and `DRAW_SUBMIT`/`DRAW_END` records show the production
  scene advanced beyond asset inventory into draw submission. They are not
  pixel evidence; without a live QMP frame, black output versus surface
  occlusion/composition remains unresolved.

  returns, third enter without return) while reaching scene-add/draw markers.
  This makes a missing static asset/scene-add explanation weaker for these runs,
  but without live QMP frames it does not resolve whether rendering is visible
  or covered by another surface.
- The reason each run-local observer/coordinator stopped before recording its
  capture result remains UNKNOWN. The current evidence gate should write each
  phase before proceeding and preserve an error stage on every exit path.

### Hypotheses

1. Trace-enabled execution correlates with the FLR-0378 silhouette; prediction:
   vehicle ROI gains achromatic edges in live QMP.
2. Trace is observational only; prediction: stage markers appear but QMP stays
   uniformly gray.
3. Trace volume changes timing/capture liveness; prediction: present/process
   evidence stalls or the live-capture gate fails, leaving visual causality
   UNKNOWN.
