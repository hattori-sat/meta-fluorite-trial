### Hypotheses

- **H1: Present is waiting inside Lavapipe/WSI.** Support: a complete selected

  Refutation: a complete selected stack remains outside the wait path.

  unmatched sample.** Support: process/thread loss before attach or a complete
  stack outside the Present path. Refutation: the live selected stack is
  directly inside the same Present wait.
- **H3: missing Sequoia pixels are a separate rendering/output boundary.** A
  stack capture does not decide this; preserve a later pixel/surface test.
