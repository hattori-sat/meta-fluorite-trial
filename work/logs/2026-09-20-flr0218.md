## Inferences

- A readback/present image-index mismatch is falsified for this run: the
  current-color image handle is identical at acquire/current-color/draw/readback
  and present reports index 0 successfully.
- Native rendering and readback are proven by the RGB-positive driver payload;
  the remaining first-divergence boundary is final Wayland/native surface
  composition, import, stacking, or display/scanout.
- The QMP capture path is not globally black because the HUD is visible; the
  missing native pixels are therefore not explained by a total QEMU capture
  failure.
- The source/layer baseline-lock synchronization was the cause of the
  regenerated historical patch; runtime 3D cause remains UNKNOWN.

## Hypotheses

1. The target bytes are real but native Wayland composition imports an empty or
   different buffer.
2. The native surface is committed but hidden, stacked below, or mapped to a
   different final output region.
3. QMP/scanout selects a different native surface than the traced compositor
   buffer; this remains possible but is not yet proven.
