# FLR-0088 — vkQueuePresentKHR wait owner

## Plan

Use FLR-0086's stable queue-present entry boundary to identify the wait owner
before changing product behavior.

## Facts

- FLR-0086's QMP and marker evidence are complete: 2D HUD visible, native
  candidate zero, queue-present return absent.
- No new QEMU is active after the QMP teardown.

## Inferences

- Static semaphore mapping plus one bounded live debugger/syscall sample has
  higher value than another scene/light experiment.

## Hypotheses

1. Application submit does not signal the semaphore waited by present.
2. llvmpipe/WSI blocks internally before returning.
3. The trace boundary is misplaced.

## UNKNOWN

- First blocking symbol and semaphore state.
