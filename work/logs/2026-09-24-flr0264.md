## Facts

- `0283-runtime` protocol evidence recorded `wl_subsurface@33` for
  `wl_surface@35`, parent `wl_surface@14`, position `(460,260)`, and
  `place_above(wl_surface@39)`.
- The readback surface performed `attach`, `damage(0,0,360,280)`, and

- The live relaunch QMP video in `0283-runtime/qmp-relaunch-frames` kept the
  2D surface visible in all ten frames, but ROI `(460,260,360,280)` was black
  in all ten frames. Full-frame SHA-256:
  `2149fccddf4ce9a725333e416f6b4ba1e0f2bf83976ac4d9daa82361c8d02046`.
- `0284-shm-control` used the same fixed image and QEMU profile with only
  `FLUORITE_NATIVE_VISIBLE_SHM_CUBE=1`. Its ten QMP frames showed the target
  ROI fully changed with `24178` chromatic pixels and a three-face cube.
  Full-frame SHA-256:
  `90c2635cf8ed1e93eb159dbd38eb00c66a3041d5b481ce18361a33840d3359c3`.
- Both QEMU runs ended through QMP quit and reported zero residual targets.

## Inference

The child surface geometry and QMP timing are not the primary failure. SHM and
QMP can display the same region in the same image. The failure is narrowed to
the readback publication update, with buffer lifecycle/ownership as the first
next discriminator.

## Hypotheses

1. Reusing a busy SHM buffer without release tracking.
2. Wayland proxy use from the readback callback thread.
3. Pixel/alpha import contract mismatch.
