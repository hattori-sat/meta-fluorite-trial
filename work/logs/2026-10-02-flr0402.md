### Inference

- A `full` region resolved from the PPM header and explicit unavailable-ROI
  records should make the postprocessor dimension-safe without weakening
  numeric bounds or changing captured pixels.

### Hypotheses

1. The fixed 1280x800 caller value is the first and complete cause of
   FLR-0401's post-run analyzer failure. Support: the synthetic 720x400
   regression failed at the fixed full ROI before code changes and passed
   after; the exact 720x400 PPM now succeeds with only Sequoia explicitly
   skipped. Refutation: any failure before ROI bounds or on valid in-bounds
   regions would indicate another defect.
2. The pre-launch PPM itself is malformed. Support would require the exact raw
   file to fail P6/header/payload validation independent of region selection.
   Refutation: its hash-verified exact replay completed full and HUD analysis
   at 720x400, so this hypothesis is rejected for FLR-0401.

### UNKNOWN

- Whether future QMP profiles emit additional dimensions. This design is based
  on parsed PPM dimensions, not an enumerated list of expected sizes.
- Product cause of black Sequoia remains outside this ticket and UNKNOWN.
