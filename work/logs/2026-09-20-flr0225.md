# FLR-0225 working log — diagnose post-input white surface and route composition

Date: 2026-09-20
Ticket: FLR-0225
Status: Done

## Outcome

The first divergence is now isolated to the Flutter-parent repaint/composition
boundary when the pointer moves into the Scenes control area. The native SHM
cube ROI does not change, the Flutter HUD/background ROI changes to uniform
white before button down/up, and no native surface reattach follows. A neutral
pointer move does not reproduce the change. This closes the diagnostic unit and
opens FLR-0226 for the minimal transparent-parent/hover correction.

## Facts

- The inherited image is the FLR-0224 Mini-built image; its rootfs,
  kernel, qemuboot, layer revision, and patch identity are recorded in the
  predecessor ticket.
- The inherited diagnostic switch is
  `FLUORITE_NATIVE_EMPTY_INPUT_REGION=1`.
- FLR-0224 initial QMP evidence contains a chromatic diagnostic ROI and HUD;
  the combined click frame has a uniform-white HUD ROI and no Scenes marker.
- QMP teardown for the predecessor run passed with no residual target process
  or QMP socket.
- In the stepwise run, the initial, x-move, down, and up frames were captured
  separately. The initial and x-only frames have SHA-256
  `830fd73a5eb8ca51798dc037add59fcb37c9e3b5c3082f4d515c487fa0ab0cf4`;
  the y-move, down, and up frames have SHA-256
  `83b474a377d3b8edb59a542f79cd2446961c0daef0fe44b734be22d15349f1fb`.
- ROI analysis shows the native cube ROI remains chromatic with 24178 pixels
  before and after the transition, while the HUD ROI changes to uniform white
  (`chromatic_pixels=0`, luma `[255,255]`).
- In the neutral-move run, all three frames have the initial SHA-256 and the
  Flutter parent remains unchanged. QMP teardown passed for both runs.
- Bounded runtime evidence shows pointer motion entering the Flutter parent
  followed by Flutter-parent attach/damage/frame/commit events, with no native
  surface attach/commit after the motion and no route marker before the white
  frame.
- Static application source confirms the Scenes action is registered on the
  button `onPressed` callback; pointer hover itself is not the route callback.
- One local harness attempt used an incorrect evidence-root path and failed to
  address its QMP socket. The path was corrected and the same QEMU run passed;
  this is recorded as a harness error, not a runtime failure.
