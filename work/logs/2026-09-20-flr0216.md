## Inferences

- The SHM compositor path and the self-made 3D-looking control fixture are
  working on the authoritative image.
- A Wayland surface update can change the final QMP frame, so the compositor
  is not globally frozen.
- The readback callback is not carrying the expected rendered geometry. The
  likely boundary is readback source/state or timing, while native WSI QMP
  visibility remains independently unresolved.

## Hypotheses

1. `renderer->readPixels` callback receives a cleared/placeholder image rather
   than the presented native swapchain image.
2. The callback payload is valid but its format/origin conversion turns it into
   uniform white; this is lower probability because the source bytes are
   uniform before conversion.
3. The earlier RGB-positive mapping belongs to a different WSI buffer or
   timing point than the callback payload. This must be correlated by buffer
   identity before any fix.
