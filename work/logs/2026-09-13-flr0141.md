### API relationship finding

- Dart `PlanetariumSceneView.getSceneShapes()` supplies the system, orbit,
  and planet shapes around `scenePosition=(-720,0,680)`. The Dart camera
  serializes `targetPosition` from that orbit point but previously serialized
  `flightStartPosition` as `(orbitDistance,0,0)`.
- Native `SceneTextDeserializer` consumes `scene.camera` and routes it to
  `CameraManager`. For `INERTIA_AND_GESTURES`, Native directly calls
  `lookAt(flightStartPosition, targetPosition, upVector)` during the initial
  camera setup. It does not interpret `flightStartPosition` as a radius or
  apply Dart's `orbitOriginPoint` and `orbitRotation` fields at that boundary.
- Therefore the prior Planetarium payload paired an eye near the origin with
  a target near `(-720,0,680)`, while the renderable shapes were also near
  `(-720,0,680)`. This is an explicit Dart/Native coordinate-contract
  mismatch. The fixture Cube did not expose it because its target was the
  origin and its radius-only value happened to be a usable world-space eye.
