## Work unit

Restore a valid positive control for the current native 3D pipeline after the
Planetarium camera contract was corrected. Add one explicit runtime diagnostic
switch, generated through Mac Yocto Devtool, that keeps the native minimal
fixture on its local/default camera while leaving the production Planetarium
camera path unchanged. Prove the result with the fixed Mini image and QMP-only
pixels.

## Problem

The current image applies the deserialized Planetarium camera to the ViewTarget.
The Dart payload is target-relative and resolves to `eye=(-640,0,680)` and
`target=(-720,0,680)`. The native minimal fixture still creates its cube around
the origin. Its current QMP-black result therefore conflates a camera-frame
mismatch with the render/present path. Historical fixture-positive evidence was
captured before this camera application was made unconditional and cannot be
blindly reused as a current-image control.
